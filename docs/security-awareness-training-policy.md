## 1. Purpose & scope

Ensure everyone with ADLA access understands their security and data-protection
responsibilities and can recognize and report threats. Applies to all staff,
officers, and contractors; content is role-appropriate.

## 2. Training requirements

| When | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Onboarding | Complete security & data-protection induction **before** receiving access (or within `[TBD]` days). |
| Annually | Refresher for all in-scope users. |
| On change | Targeted training when threats, tools, or policies change materially. |
| Role-specific | Extra training for privileged roles (`admin`, `legal_unit`, `schedule_officer`) on PII handling and officer scoping. |

## 3. Core topics

- ADLA data sensitivity and the **data-classification** tiers (handling
  Restricted-PII: national-ID, Ghana Card images).
- **Acceptable use**, least privilege, need-to-know, no curiosity-browsing.
- **Credential hygiene**, MFA `[TBD]`, phishing/social-engineering awareness.
- **Data-protection basics** (Act 843): lawful handling, data-subject rights
  (DSAR), breach indicators.
- **Incident & event reporting (6.8):** how and when to report — fast,
  no-blame, via the defined channel (IRP §5.2).
- Physical security & **clear desk** for offices handling forms.
- Secure-development training for engineers (secure-coding standard).
