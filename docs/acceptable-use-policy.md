## 1. Purpose & scope

Define acceptable use of ADLA, its data, accounts, and supporting devices.
Applies to all staff, officers (`schedule_officer`, `legal_unit`, `admin`),
contractors, and anyone granted privileged or operational access. Applicants
(public users) are governed by the public Terms, not this policy.

## 2. General rules

- Use ADLA and its data **only** for authorized official purposes (processing
  asset declarations under Article 286(5)).
- Access **only** the data your role and assignment require (least privilege /
  need-to-know). Schedule Officers act only within their assigned
  `CollectionOffice`(s).
- Do **not** browse, copy, export, share, or alter applicant data beyond what a
  task requires. Curiosity browsing of PII is prohibited.
- All actions are **audit-logged**; expect accountability.

## 3. Protecting credentials & access

- Keep your password and tokens confidential; never share accounts.
- Use a strong, unique password; enable MFA where available `[TBD]`.
- Lock your screen when away; log out on shared devices.
- Report suspected credential compromise immediately (IRP §5.2).

## 4. Handling personal data (PII)

- Treat national-ID numbers and Ghana Card images as **Restricted-PII**
  (data-classification policy): never copy into email/SMS, chat, personal
  devices, screenshots, or unmanaged storage.
- Do not download/export PII except via authorized features, for authorized
  purposes, to authorized recipients.
- Physical declaration forms/printouts: keep secured; follow clear-desk
  practice; dispose of securely.

## 5. Acceptable device & network use

- Access ADLA from authorized, secured devices; keep them patched and
  malware-protected.
- Do not install unauthorized software or disable security controls.
- Do not connect ADLA processing to untrusted networks without protection
  (see remote-working policy).

## 6. Prohibited

- Sharing accounts/credentials; circumventing access controls or rate limits.
- Unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or deletion of data.
- Using ADLA data for personal, commercial, political, or any non-official
  purpose.
- Attempting to attack, scan, or test ADLA without written authorization.
- Storing ADLA Restricted-PII on personal devices/accounts/cloud.

## 7. Monitoring & enforcement

Use is monitored and audit-logged. Violations may lead to disciplinary action
(HR security policy) and, where unlawful, legal consequences under Act 843/1038.
