## Confidentiality Agreement

**Parties.** This Agreement is between **`[Controller / employing entity]`**
("the Organization") and **`[Full name]`** ("the Recipient"),
`[role/contractor]`.

**1. Confidential Information.** Includes all personal data and information
processed by the Asset Declaration Portal (ADLA), in particular:
- National-identity (Ghana Card) numbers and **Ghana Card images**;
- Applicant names, contact details, and **asset-declaration records**;
- Authentication credentials, cryptographic keys, and security configurations;
- Any non-public operational, technical, or security information about ADLA.

**2. Obligations.** The Recipient shall:
- Access and use Confidential Information **only** for authorized official duties
  and only to the extent their role requires (least privilege / need-to-know);
- **Not disclose** it to any unauthorized person, inside or outside the
  Organization;
- **Not copy, remove, store, or transmit** it except via authorized systems for
  authorized purposes (no personal devices/accounts/cloud);
- Protect it in line with the Acceptable Use, Data Classification, and Information
  Security policies;
- **Report** any suspected loss, unauthorized access, or breach immediately
  (incident-response process).

**3. Legal framework.** The Recipient acknowledges that misuse of personal data
may constitute an offence under the **Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)** and
related law, and may attract criminal and civil liability.

**4. Duration.** These obligations apply during engagement and **continue
indefinitely after** it ends, for as long as the information remains
confidential.

**5. Return / deletion.** On termination or request, the Recipient shall return
or securely destroy all Confidential Information and assets in their possession
and surrender any access/credentials/keys.

**6. Consequences of breach.** Breach may result in disciplinary action up to
dismissal, contract termination, and legal proceedings.

**Acknowledgement**

I have read and understood this Agreement and agree to be bound by it.
